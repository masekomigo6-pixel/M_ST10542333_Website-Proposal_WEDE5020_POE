Pickup

Place your order online and pick it up at our store at your convenience.

Pickup Location: 123 Main Street, Johannesburg

Ordering Policy

  • Orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Payment is required at the time of order placement.
  • We accept credit/debit cards, instant EFT, and cash on pickup (for pickup orders only).

  • Allergy information: Please inform us of any allergies when placing your order.
