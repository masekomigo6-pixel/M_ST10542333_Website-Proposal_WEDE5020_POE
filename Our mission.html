Our Projects

Making a difference in our communities

🌿 Community Gardens

Growing green spaces together

Our community garden project transforms vacant urban spaces into thriving green areas where residents can grow food, connect with nature, and build community bonds. These gardens serve as gathering places, educational spaces, and sources of fresh, healthy produce.

Key Achievements:

  • 5 community gardens established across Johannesburg
  • 200+ community members actively engaged
  • 500+ kg of fresh produce grown annually
  • 20+ workshops on sustainable gardening

📚 Environmental Education

Inspiring the next generation

Our environmental education program brings hands-on learning to schools and communities, teaching children about ecology, conservation, and sustainable living. We believe that education is the foundation for lasting environmental change.

Key Achievements:

  • 15 partner schools in the program
  • 50+ workshops delivered annually
  • 1,500+ students reached
  • 30+ educators trained in environmental education

♻️ Recycling Initiative

Reducing waste, protecting our planet

Our recycling initiative promotes waste reduction and sustainable living through community recycling programs, educational workshops, and plastic reduction campaigns. We work with local businesses and residents to create a circular economy.

Key Achievements:

  • 5 community recycling hubs established
  • 10+ recycling workshops conducted
  • 2,000+ kg of waste recycled annually
  • 5 plastic reduction campaigns completed
  • Reduced community waste by 15%
