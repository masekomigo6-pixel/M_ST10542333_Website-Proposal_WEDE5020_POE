About Green Future

Founded in 2018, Green Future is a local non-profit organization dedicated to urban greening and environmental education. We believe every community deserves a green, healthy living environment. Through community gardens, environmental curricula, and sustainability advocacy, we are turning this vision into reality across Johannesburg and beyond.
